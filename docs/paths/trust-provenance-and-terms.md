# Reading path: trust, provenance and terms

What is signed, what is computed, and why the difference matters. Each step says what it adds to the one before; the pages
themselves hold the detail.

1. [How polis thinks about identity and trust](../signet/overview.md): *signed things travel; judgments don't.*
2. [Assertions](../signet/concepts/assertions.md): the four things a person can sign: attribution, licence, attestation and counter-signature.
3. [Projection](../signet/concepts/projection.md): signed is portable and permanent; computed is per-viewer and disposable.
4. [Verification](../signet/concepts/verification.md): facts versus judgments, and what a verifier may say.
5. [Trust](../signet/concepts/trust.md): why the follow graph is signed; [recipe 14](../signet/recipes/14-verify-signed-edges.md) checks a follow file by hand.
6. [The graph](../signet/concepts/graph.md): the trust triangle, end to end.
7. [Policy and licence](../general/concepts/policy-and-licence.md): the two kinds of rule a site states, pointing in opposite directions. The next two steps take one each.
8. [Licence](../signet/spec/license.md), then [Set your terms](../signet/guides/set-your-terms.md): terms are **outbound**, what others may do with your work.
9. [Policy grammar](../general/reference/policy-grammar.md), then [In defense of bless](../general/reference/in-defense-of-bless.md): policy is **inbound**, who may reach you. *Same noun as a licence, opposite direction.*
10. [Attestation](../signet/spec/attestation.md), then [Make a claim](../signet/guides/attest.md), then recipes [2, pin a correction](../signet/recipes/02-pin-a-correction.md) and [9, prove when](../signet/recipes/09-prove-when.md).
11. [Delegation](../signet/concepts/delegation.md), then [Who blessed this?](../signet/guides/who-blessed-this.md): was it the person, or their agent, and under what grant?
