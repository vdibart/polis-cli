Rotate Keys

Key rotation generates a new Ed25519 signing keypair for your site. Your old key is backed up and previously signed content remains verifiable via key history.

When to rotate
  • You suspect your private key has been compromised
  • You're migrating your site to a new machine
  • Periodic hygiene (once a year is plenty)

You don't need to rotate frequently. If your keys are safe, there's no urgency to change them.
